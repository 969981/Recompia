# Legal

## No game files

Recompia ships no game files, no keys and no tools to dump or decrypt a game. To play, you need your own extracted copy of the game.

## Not affiliated

Recompia is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK inc., The Pokémon Company or Koei Tecmo. All trademarks belong to their owners.

## Rights holders

Write to **legal.recompia@gmail.com**. A notice should include:

- the work you hold the rights to
- the exact file or URL in this project
- your contact details
- a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised

Content that matches a valid notice is removed from the repository and from the next release. The person who uploaded it may send a
counter-notice to the same address.

## Mods

- Mods the launcher lists or carries contain no third-party media (music, voice clips, video, official art) and no game files. Patch
  mods carry only the differences.
- Every listed mod states a licence that covers all of its assets.
- A listing is removed on a valid notice.

## No piracy

Requests for game files, keys or dumps, and links to them or to ROM sites, are not allowed in issues, pull requests or the project's
community spaces. They are deleted.
